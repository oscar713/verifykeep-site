The five checks

Every statement has to prove itself.

Each check shows the actual numbers and the arithmetic behind it, so you can see why a statement passed, not just that it did.

  • A

    Balance proof

    Opening balance + deposits − withdrawals must equal the closing balance printed on the statement. To the cent.

  • B

    Transaction count

    The number of rows extracted must match the statement.

  • C

    Deposit & withdrawal totals

    Extracted totals are compared with the totals the bank printed.

  • D

    Completeness

    Every page is read and every date falls inside the statement period.

  • E

    Anomalies

    Unusual or low-confidence rows are flagged and highlighted for your review.

The export gate

If it doesn't balance, it doesn't leave.

  • Unverified files are locked. Export stays blocked until the statement verifies.
  • Exceptions are deliberate. Only a firm administrator can override, and every override is logged.
  • No silent fixes. Amounts are never adjusted to force a balance. Rows missing a date, amount or direction stop the export instead of disappearing.
  • Unsure? It says so. Low-confidence rows are flagged and highlighted, right next to the original PDF.

Questions, answered

What does VerifyKeep export?

CSV files in three layouts: QuickBooks Online, Xero and a generic CSV for any spreadsheet. You import the file into your accounting software; VerifyKeep does not connect to or sync with QuickBooks or Xero directly.

What happens when a statement doesn't balance?

The export is blocked and the validation report shows exactly which check failed and by how much. You can review the flagged rows next to the original PDF. Only a firm administrator can grant an exception, and every exception is written to the audit log.

Can VerifyKeep change my numbers to make a statement balance?

No. Amounts are never edited to force a balance, and amounts aren't editable in the app. You can edit descriptions, which re-runs validation. Rows missing a date, amount or direction stop the export instead of being dropped.

How long do you keep my statements?

You choose per client: delete 24 hours after export, keep for 30 days (the default) or keep for 90 days. Firm administrators can also delete a statement whenever it isn't being processed.
