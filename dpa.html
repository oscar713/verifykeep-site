Data Processing Agreement (draft)

Draft, not in effect. VerifyKeep is not yet accepting customer accounts, and this DPA has not been reviewed by an attorney. We publish it so firms can see the terms we intend to offer. It binds no one until a final version is posted with an effective date and presented for acceptance at signup. The open items at the end of this page must be resolved first.

This draft DPA would apply when your firm (the controller) uses VerifyKeep (the processor) to extract and verify data from bank statements you upload. The processor will be the legal entity named here before launch; that name and its mailing address are still to be added.

  • 1. Roles and scope

    Your firm is the controller; VerifyKeep is the processor. We would process personal data in uploaded statements (account holder names, account numbers and transaction details about your clients and the people they transact with) only to extract, normalize, reconcile and export transaction data for you, and to operate and secure the service.

    Account details for your own users and billing contacts are handled under our Privacy notice, not this DPA.

  • 2. Documented instructions

    We process customer data only on your documented instructions, chiefly your uploads, settings and in-app actions. If we believe an instruction would violate applicable law, we will tell you promptly and will not act on it until it is resolved.

  • 3. Confidentiality and personnel

    VerifyKeep is currently operated by one person, who is the only individual with administrative access to production systems. Before anyone else is given access to customer data, they will sign a written confidentiality agreement, and their access will be limited to what their role requires and removed when they leave.

  • 4. Technical and organizational measures

    The application is built with the controls below, which are described further on the Security page. They are implemented and tested in code; they have not been independently audited.

    • Statement files encrypted at rest in Amazon S3 with AWS KMS keys; traffic encrypted in transit with TLS
    • PostgreSQL row-level security that keeps each workspace's data separate at the database layer
    • TOTP two-factor authentication required for workspace administrators before administrative actions such as billing, member management, retention changes and statement deletion
    • Rate limits and lockouts on sign-in and two-factor codes, and security-event flags for login bursts, cross-workspace access attempts and unusual export volume
    • An append-only audit log of workspace actions
    • Scanned-page processing isolated in a separate process with time and memory limits

  • 5. Subprocessors

    You authorize the following subprocessors:

    • Amazon Web Services, Inc.: hosting, database, file storage, transactional email (Amazon SES) and OCR of scanned pages (Amazon Textract). Region: us-east-2 (Ohio, United States).
    • OpenAI: structured extraction of transactions from statement text. United States. OpenAI states that API data is not used for training by default and may be kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring (OpenAI enterprise privacy).
    • Stripe, Inc.: subscription payments. Stripe receives your firm's billing details, not statement data.

    We use each provider under its standard data processing terms. We will post any new subprocessor on this page and email workspace administrators at least 14 days before it receives customer data. If you object, you can close your workspace and we will delete your data as described in section 8.

  • 6. Requests from individuals

    We will help you respond to access, deletion and portability requests for data we process for you. Administrators can export statements and delete them in the app. If an individual contacts us directly about data we process for you, we will forward the request to you rather than answer it ourselves, unless law requires otherwise.

  • 7. Security incident notice

    If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting data we process for you, we will email your workspace administrators without undue delay. We will not wait until our investigation is finished. The first notice will say what we know at that point, and we will send updates as we learn more: what happened, the data and approximate number of records affected, likely consequences, the steps we have taken and a contact for questions. The final version of this DPA will state a maximum notice time.

  • 8. Return and deletion

    While a statement is kept, you can export it as CSV, QuickBooks Online-formatted CSV, Xero-formatted CSV or OFX. Exports require a verified statement or an administrator-approved override.

    Each client has a retention setting: delete 24 hours after first export (or 90 days after processing if never exported), keep 30 days (the default) or keep 90 days. Administrators can delete a finished statement at any time.

    When the last member of a workspace deletes their account (paid plans must be cancelled first), we delete uploaded files and extracted data and anonymize the audit log. Copies can remain for a limited time: encrypted database backups expire within 14 days, and OpenAI may keep API inputs for up to 30 days as noted in section 5. Stripe keeps billing records under its own terms.

  • 9. Compliance information

    Once a year, or after a security incident, we will answer a reasonable written security questionnaire and provide this DPA and a summary of our controls. We do not currently hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report.

  • 10. Location and international transfers

    VerifyKeep stores and processes customer data in the United States. The service is designed for US firms. We do not currently offer EU or UK transfer terms. If your firm is subject to the GDPR or UK GDPR, contact us before uploading statements.

  • 11. Liability and precedence

    Liability under this DPA will follow the limitation of liability in the final Terms of Service, which have not been published yet. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on a data processing matter, this DPA controls.

  • 12. Acceptance and contact

    When final, this DPA will be presented with the Terms of Service at signup, and acceptance will be recorded with your account. Questions: hello@verifykeep.com.

Open items before this DPA takes effect: legal entity name and mailing address; attorney review of this DPA, the Terms of Service and the Privacy notice; final Terms with a limitation of liability; recorded acceptance at signup; confirmation of OpenAI account data controls and the AWS AI-services opt-out for Textract; a maximum breach-notice time; and a decision on EU and UK transfer terms.
