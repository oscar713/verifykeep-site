Data Processing Agreement

This DPA applies when your firm (the controller) uses VerifyKeep (the processor) to extract and verify data from bank statements you upload. It is part of the Terms of Service and takes effect when you create an account.

  • 1. Roles and scope

    Your firm is the data controller; VerifyKeep is the data processor. We process personal data contained in uploaded bank statements — account holder names, account numbers, and transaction details — solely to extract, normalize, reconcile, and export transaction data for you, and to operate and secure the service.

    Processing continues for as long as your account exists and per your chosen retention setting, then until deletion completes as described in section 8.

  • 2. Documented instructions

    We process data only on your documented instructions — chiefly your uploads and in-app actions — plus what the Terms and this DPA require. If an instruction would violate applicable law, we tell you before acting on it.

  • 3. Confidentiality

    Everyone at VerifyKeep who can reach customer data is bound by confidentiality obligations. Access is limited to what each person needs to do their job, logged in an append-only audit trail, and reviewed on change of role or departure.

  • 4. Technical and organizational measures

    We maintain the controls described on the Security page, including:

    • Encryption of statement files at rest (AWS S3 SSE-KMS) and of all traffic in transit (TLS)
    • PostgreSQL row-level security enforcing workspace isolation at the database layer
    • Required TOTP multi-factor authentication for administrative actions
    • Rate limiting, login lockouts, and automated detection of anomalous access
    • Append-only audit logging of workspace actions
    • Bounded, time- and memory-limited statement processing

  • 5. Subprocessors

    You authorize us to use the following categories of subprocessor: cloud hosting and object storage (AWS), email delivery for transactional messages, payment processing (Stripe), and a structured-extraction AI API for statement parsing under no-training API terms.

    We impose data-protection obligations on each subprocessor at least as protective as this DPA and remain liable for their acts. Material new subprocessors are announced on this page before adoption; you may object within 14 days.

  • 6. Data subject requests

    We assist you in answering access, correction, deletion, and portability requests for data we process for you — including the self-service account deletion built into Settings. If a data subject contacts us directly, we redirect them to you unless law requires otherwise.

  • 7. Personal data breach notice

    We notify you without undue delay after confirming a personal data breach affecting data we process for you, with what happened, the data affected, and mitigation steps. Administrative contact for breach notices is the workspace admin email on file.

  • 8. Return and deletion

    Your data is exportable at any time in CSV, QBO, Xero, or OFX format. Statements are deleted per your per-client retention setting (after export, 30 days, or 90 days). Closing your account deletes uploaded files and extracted data; audit entries are anonymized so the log survives without your personal data. Stripe retains billing records under its own terms.

  • 9. Audits and compliance evidence

    We make available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance — this DPA, the Security page, our automated test suite, and audit-log exports. On-site audits are replaced by written questionnaires once per year; additional review happens where legally required.

  • 10. International transfers

    Statement processing runs in AWS US regions. Where data protection law requires a transfer mechanism, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses are incorporated by reference as the transfer safeguard.

  • 11. Liability and precedence

    Liability under this DPA follows the limitation in the Terms of Service. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms, this DPA wins for processing matters.

  • 12. Contact and signature

    Questions and requests: hello@verifykeep.com. Because this DPA is accepted with the Terms at signup, no separate signature is needed — your account creation is your signature, and ours.

Template, not legal advice: this DPA is written for the common controller–processor relationship. If your firm operates under a specific regime (GDPR, UK GDPR, state privacy laws), have counsel review this template against your obligations.
